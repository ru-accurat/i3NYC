    What changed in 2026

    Four rules every Italian company should know

    Federal changes in 2025–26 closed some doors to foreign-owned firms and opened others. Plan the entity and the cap table before you apply.

    SBA loans: closed to foreign ownersSince March 1, 2026, SBA 7(a) and 504 loans require 100% ownership by US citizens living in the US. Italian-owned companies are not eligible.
    SBIR/STTR: stricter screeningReauthorized on April 13, 2026 through September 30, 2031, with new foreign-ownership and foreign-ties review. Only majority-US-owned companies qualify.
    H-1B: $100,000 feeNew petitions for workers outside the US carry a $100,000 fee since September 21, 2025 (under litigation). Early-stage hiring from Italy becomes costly.
    E-2 and O-1: the founder routesItaly is an E-2 treaty country (applications through the US Embassy in Rome, allow about 3 months). O-1A has no cap and suits founders with awards, press or funding.

    Landing in 7 steps

    From Italy to an operating Tri-State presence

    A prepared company typically needs six to nine months. Run Italian financing and US program applications in parallel.

    1. Month 0–1Readiness check

      Test the US thesis, confirm revenue or capital, and choose the executive who will relocate. Landing pads ask for about $5M revenue or capital to hire 5+ locally.

    2. Month 1–2Italian financing

      Apply to SIMEST Misura Stati Uniti. Startups add the ICE Global Start Up Program in its May call.

    3. Month 1–2Pick the state

      Meet NYCEDC, NJEDA or Connecticut Innovations. Compare incentives, talent and where your first customers sit.

    4. Month 2–4Apply to a program

      Landing pad, accelerator or pilot. Bring a local hiring plan, pilot targets and proof of capital.

    5. Month 2–5Entity and visas

      Incorporate the US subsidiary, open banking, and file E-2 in Rome or O-1. Register for state taxes.

    6. Month 5–9Land

      Start the cohort, sign a lease, and file relocation tax credits inside their deadlines. Hire first local staff.

    7. Month 9+Scale

      Raise a US round with state co-investment, use job-creation credits and turn pilots into contracts.
